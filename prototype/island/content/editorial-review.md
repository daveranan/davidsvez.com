# Independent editorial review

This review covers the public text in all 283 project records, the site-level copy and three published essays, the compact Lightkeeper override, and the intentionally playful world dialogue. It distinguishes case studies from collections, media artifacts, and personal essays.

## Review standard

- Lead case summaries with the problem, the author's intervention, and the intended use or benefit.
- Do not present intent as a measured outcome. No new efficiency, adoption, revenue, or conversion claims without source evidence.
- Preserve role, collaborator, agency, date, prototype, and implementation boundaries.
- Give media artifacts useful captions without inventing a full design question or success story.
- Remove public-facing recovery notes, authoring TODOs, and repetitive descriptions of what a folder contains.
- Keep deliberate personal/art-buffer language and optional NPC banter; professional case copy need not erase the site's voice.

## Baseline findings sent to the rewriting agent

1. UserWise and Flax describe the case-study sequence or artifacts before explaining the authoring/editor workflow problem.
2. Internal statuses such as `ARCHIVE READY`, `MEDIA RECOVERY NEEDED`, and requests to recover PRs/dates do not belong in public presentation.
3. Daedalus's two posts have different observed view counts. Their framing is a plausible lesson, not controlled proof of why one performed better.
4. The Venice record concerns White Mirror's Production Bridge presentation; it must not become a claim that Daedalus Alpha screened at the festival.
5. Fox Stories' trailer NPC interactions were scripted. Prototype footage must not imply those systems were implemented.

7. The Daedalus blog's hypothetical comparison of a 20-person/six-month team with a three-person/two-month experiment is not demonstrated productivity evidence.
8. KCD Combat lacks a recorded design question. Describe the surviving prototype without inventing one.
9. Archive leaves currently use generic `VIDEO archive artifact` wording; labels should identify the visible content instead.
10. Generic `DESIGN / DEVELOPMENT` role metadata on every deep media leaf can misrepresent the artifact. Prefer the verified parent credit or omit generic metadata in the artifact view.
